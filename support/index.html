Common questions

Where is my journal stored?
Only on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.

How do I move my journal to a new iPhone?
Restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. You can also export your journal as text from Settings.

I turned on Face ID lock and it won't open.
Tap Unlock to try again. If Face ID fails, iOS lets you use your iPhone passcode instead.

How do I delete everything?
Go to Settings → Erase everything, or delete the app.
