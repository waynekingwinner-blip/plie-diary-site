Privacy Policy

Plié Diary · Last updated October 14, 2026

Plié Diary ("the app") is a ballet class journal for iPhone. We built it so that your journal belongs to you alone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

We collect no data

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no account and no login, it contains no analytics or advertising software, and it does not send anything you enter to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive your information.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter — your name, class entries, notes, corrections, reflections, steps and progress — is stored only on your device. Your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup) may include this data; those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Photos, videos, camera and microphone

If you attach photos or videos to a class, or capture them with the camera, the app saves a copy only inside the app on your iPhone. The microphone is used only to record sound with videos you capture. Nothing is uploaded.

Face ID

If you turn on the journal lock, the app asks iOS to confirm it is you with Face ID or your passcode. The check happens entirely within iOS. The app never receives or stores your face data.

Exporting and deleting

You can export your journal as text from Settings. The export goes only where you choose to send it. You can erase all of your data at any time from Settings → Erase everything, or by deleting the app.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the "Last updated" date above. If a future version of the app ever begins to collect any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
