Plié Diary

A private journal for your ballet classes

Plié Diary is an iPhone app for dancers who want to remember what happened in class. Log each class in seconds: the steps you worked on, your teacher's corrections, how it felt, and photos or videos of your practice.

Look back through a calendar and a searchable journal, learn the vocabulary in a built-in step dictionary, and watch your hours, classes and streaks add up over time.

Everything you write stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in and no tracking.
